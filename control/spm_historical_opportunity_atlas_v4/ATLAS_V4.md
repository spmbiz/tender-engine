## Strategic implication

The broad Australia `ICT_DIGITAL_TALENT` finding was not an artefact of one generic label. It decomposes into many individually recurring roles, and supplier concentration remains low across nearly every role. This substantially strengthens the **public-sector contractor/talent brokerage** thesis.

The most interesting combinations for further historical buyer/winner analysis are:

- Business Analysts;
- Software Developers/Engineers;
- Data Analysts + Data Engineers;
- Technical Writers;
- Project/Program Managers;
- Agile/Delivery roles;
- Test/QA.

Cyber Security and Architecture remain attractive historically but may carry materially more clearance/security/specialist burden.

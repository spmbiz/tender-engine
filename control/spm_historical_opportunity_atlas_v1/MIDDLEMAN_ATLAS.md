# SPM Historical Middleman Atlas v1

Scope: strict historical **notice-first** expansion over the 2,250,547 Global Core records. No live tender and no DCE is used. This file focuses on procurements whose wording explicitly describes brokerage/resale/staffing/fulfilment or standardized-goods supply rather than merely inferring that SPM could subcontract it.

The expansion matched **3,897 records** across six strict intermediary families. Countries/currency buckets remain separate; no FX normalization is applied.

## Guardrails

- OPEN_BID scores use OPEN_PUBLIC historical rows only.
- DIRECT_BUYER scores are relationship/outbound evidence, not evidence of an open tender or low competition.
- Invitation, reserved/regional, framework/DPS and competitive-other routes remain separate.

- Historical evidence never satisfies live eligibility/DCE gates.
- Title-led classifier favors precision over recall.

# SPM Historical Opportunity Atlas v2

**Scope: historical structured evidence only. No live tender and no DCE is used.**

This atlas ranks **historical market structures worth further archive analysis**, not current bidability. Two evidence classes are kept separate:

- `NOTICE_FIRST`: historical tender/competition structure can be analyzed from notices and linked awards where available.
- `AWARD_FIRST`: buyer demand, historical value and supplier fragmentation are valid; open-competition ease is **not** inferred.

Currencies are never mixed. Native-currency values are shown only inside each market row.

## Historical holds / anti-patterns

These are useful because they show why volume or ticket alone is insufficient:

- **Australia media buying**: historical median ~AUD 6.57m but linked historical supplier concentration was 100% in the reviewed cohort → `HOLD_CONCENTRATION`.
- **Germany postal delivery**: 8 corrected records / 5 buyers / median ~EUR 2.94m but **top supplier share 76.9%** → structurally much less attractive than France postal fulfilment.
- **Québec postal delivery**: 21 /14 buyers /5 repeat / median CAD400k but top supplier share **50%**; bidder=1 is not interpreted as easy competition.
- **Health/clinical staffing** is deliberately not promoted despite large markets because professional/clinical staffing burden is qualitatively different from IT/admin staffing.
- USA/Australia hardware/manufacturing-coded false semantic matches remain in the archive but are not promoted merely because an IT/data phrase matched their text.

## What materially changed from Atlas v1

1. **Standardized consumables became a first-class thesis.** France cleaning/hygiene supply is both huge and exceptionally fragmented historically.
2. **Human-capital brokerage became much larger.** AusTender decomposition found 22,937 staffing/support awards, including 4,328 ICT/digital talent awards with 1,029 suppliers and only 2.4% top share.
3. **USA support/BPO is a major market**, not a tiny edge: 110,880 scoped awards across 1,937 submarkets. Office admin, acquisition support, IT systems design, program support and data processing have broad buyer/supplier structure.
4. **Software resale is real but heterogeneous.** UK/Canada generic reseller slices look structurally more fragmented than Netherlands/Sweden large-framework markets. Vendor-specific lanes often show materially higher concentration.
5. **Document fulfilment is stronger than print alone.** France print-insert-post and postal delivery both show 100+ buyers and low top-supplier concentration.

# Manual QA — Historical Print DCE Business Brief v1

This note is the interpretation authority for the two currently gate-ready print DCEs. The machine report remains an extraction aid; ambiguous percentage candidates must not be promoted without context review.

## 1. CNAV — Guide du nouveau retraité

Authoritative retrieved file: `1609888.resp-BPU.xlsx` from the buyer's AchatPublic consultation route.

Evidence-supported observations:
- BPU covers **impression + façonnage + livraison**.
- Pricing is requested globally for ordered quantities, not as a single flat unit-price assumption.
- Quantity bands visible in the BPU include **5,000; 10,000; 20,000; 80,000; 120,000; 160,000; 180,000** copies (and potentially additional rows outside the extracted context).
- Delivery pricing distinguishes **one** vs **two** delivery points in metropolitan France.
- The BPU references a four-year quantity horizon.

No strict evidence hit in the retrieved BPU alone established a minimum turnover, comparable-reference minimum, mandatory named team/CV, mandatory insurance, subcontracting restriction, mandatory sample/proof, or FSC/PEFC requirement. **Those gates remain UNKNOWN**, not absent, because only the retrieved authoritative BPU is evidenced here and the full consultation package may contain additional requirements.

Business implication: this is strong evidence that some public print buying can be decomposed into a supplier-quotable production/logistics matrix — exactly the shape suitable for broker price arbitrage — but it is not evidence that SPM would have passed the full candidature gates.

## 2. Communauté de communes du Pays de l'Ourcq — supports de communication

Authoritative retrieved package: `RC.zip` from AchatPublic.

Evidence-supported observations:
- Candidature asks for a **list of principal services performed during the previous three years**, with amount, date and public/private recipient; delivery/service evidence may be supported by recipient attestations or, failing that, operator declaration.
- **Insurance attestations** are to be produced under the CCAP conditions.
- **Samples are mandatory** for each lot. Samples are free, must be sent/delivered before the offer deadline, and the consultation states that a proposal may be rejected if the candidate did not send the sample.
- The main award criterion **Price = 60%**.
- The main criterion **Quality of services based on samples and proposed means = 15%**.
- Within that 15% quality criterion, the two subcriteria are **sample quality = 50% of the criterion** and **proposed means = 50% of the criterion**. Therefore the raw machine candidate `Quality/technical = 50%` is **NOT an overall award weight** and must not be quoted as such.
- Another main criterion visible in the context is **execution deadlines = 5%**. The remaining main-criterion weighting is not asserted here without a complete contextual extraction.

Business implication: this scope is brokerable operationally, but not frictionless. A viable middleman workflow must pre-arrange a printer able to produce compliant physical samples fast enough for the bid deadline, provide the required insurance evidence, and support SPM's candidature/reference strategy. Historical market attractiveness alone does not solve these gates.

## Global rule

These are two historical examples, not prevalence estimates. They refine execution hypotheses only. For any live opportunity, mandatory gates are re-read from the current DCE and missing evidence remains `UNKNOWN`.

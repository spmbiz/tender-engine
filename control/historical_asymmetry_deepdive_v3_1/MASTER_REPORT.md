## Strategic framework

Rank future archive discoveries by mechanism:

1. **Information → output** — automate human information work.
2. **Demand → human** — aggregate distributed experts/freelancers/providers.
3. **Specification → SKU** — source standardized products better/cheaper.
4. **Need → specialist deliverable** — orchestrate credentialed experts while automating research/admin/reporting.

This is deliberately broader than a fixed tender ontology and is the preferred discovery framework for finding businesses SPM had not previously considered.

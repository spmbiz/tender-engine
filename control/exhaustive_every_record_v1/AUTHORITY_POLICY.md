# Historical intelligence authority policy

This project now has two complementary historical intelligence layers.

1. **Exhaustive Every-Record v1** is authoritative for corpus accounting, source/grain coverage, row-level processing completeness, native-field completeness, broad-family inventory, residual census, and discovery of candidate lanes. It must preserve UNKNOWN and source grain.
2. **Market Intelligence v9 Final QA** remains authoritative for **commercial claims about the known named lean lanes** because its semantic classifier was manually hardened against observed false positives and procurement-route confusion.

Do not replace v9 named-lane conclusions with a higher count from the broader exhaustive classifier unless the broader cohort receives equivalent semantic QA. Exhaustive open-world/residual candidates are hypothesis generators until promoted through QA.

USAspending and AusTender are award-first procurement evidence. Never describe their rows as reconstructed original live tender notices or use their bidder/route fields as if they were notice-first competition evidence.

Historical intelligence never converts missing live eligibility evidence into PASS. DCE/PDF resolution remains the mandatory downstream gate for references, turnover, certifications, insurance, language, onsite obligations, team requirements, pricing forms, subcontracting rules, and other mandatory conditions.
